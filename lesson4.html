Math

Multi-Digit Multiplication, Division, and Factors

Building on multiplication, you will now solve division problems. For example, if one class has 24 students they put 4 students in each group, how many groups are there in total? You will also learn about factors and multiples. Factors are numbers you divied a number with nothing left over and multiples are things that you count by to get that number.

Reading

Summarizing, Inference, and Comparing Ideas

After mastering summaries and inferences, you will now compare information across paragraphs. For example, reading two paragraphs about different animals, you can compare how they find food or care for their young. If a pargraph says,"During the first several weeks, the queen provides milk, warmth, and constant grooming, which also stimulates the kittens' elimination since they cannot do so on their own. As the kittens grow, the mother starts the weaning process and teaches them crucial life skills, such as how to use a litter box and interact with others." and, "For dogs, mothers provide their puppies with milk, warmth, and protection, especially in the first several weeks. As the puppies grow, the mother dog starts the weaning process and teaches crucial life skills, including bite inhibition and communication." you can compare how they take care of there young, Both cat and dog mothers provide essential care like nursing and grooming for their young, but they teach different skills as their offspring mature.

Science

Moon Phases, Tides, Gravity, and Eclipses

Now we connect moon phases, tides, and gravity with even bigger space patterns. You will learn how solar and lunar eclipses occur when Earth, Moon, and Sun align. You will explore how the Moon’s gravity, along with the Sun, creates spring and neap tides. Understanding orbits and eclipses helps explain why these events happen regularly and how planets, moons, and stars interact in space. This builds a foundation for learning about our solar system and space science.
