Math

Multi-Digit Multiplication, Division, and Factors

Building on multiplication and division, you will now solve multi-digit problems in real-life contexts. For example, if one class has 24 students and each student brings 15 pencils, how many pencils are there in total? You will also learn about factors and multiples to find numbers that divide evenly into others. Understanding these helps with problem solving, patterns, and preparing for more advanced math topics like fractions and decimals.

Reading

Summarizing, Inference, and Comparing Ideas

After mastering summaries and inferences, you will now compare information across paragraphs. For example, reading two paragraphs about different animals, you can compare how they find food or care for their young. You will also learn to summarize longer texts in your own words and support your answers with evidence from the text. This skill helps with critical thinking and understanding more complex stories and informational texts.

Science

Moon Phases, Tides, Gravity, and Eclipses

Now we connect moon phases, tides, and gravity with even bigger space patterns. You will learn how solar and lunar eclipses occur when Earth, Moon, and Sun align. You will explore how the Moon’s gravity, along with the Sun, creates spring and neap tides. Understanding orbits and eclipses helps explain why these events happen regularly and how planets, moons, and stars interact in space. This builds a foundation for learning about our solar system and space science.
