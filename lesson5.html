Sparkle Learners: Everything You Learned So Far

Math: Multi-Digit Multiplication, Division, and Factors

In math, you have learned how to add and subtract larger numbers. Now you also know how to multiply and divide multi-digit numbers using place value strategies. You can solve real-life problems, like figuring out how many pencils 24 students bring if each brings 15 pencils. You also learned about factors and multiples, which are numbers that divide evenly into other numbers or are multiples of them. Understanding these concepts helps you prepare for fractions, decimals, and more advanced problem solving.

Reading: Summarizing, Inference, and Comparing Ideas

In reading, you began by identifying the main idea and supporting details in paragraphs. You then learned how to summarize longer texts in your own words and make inferences by using clues in the text to figure out information that isn’t directly stated. You also practiced comparing ideas across paragraphs, like how different animals find food or care for their young. These skills help you understand texts deeply, think critically, and explain your ideas clearly.

Science: Moon Phases, Tides, Gravity, and Eclipses

In science, you explored the Moon and its phases, learning how the Sun lights up different parts of the Moon. You saw how the Moon’s gravity affects ocean tides, and how the Sun’s gravity also contributes to spring and neap tides. You also learned about eclipses — solar and lunar — and how the alignment of the Sun, Moon, and Earth creates these events. Finally, you studied how gravity keeps the Moon in orbit around Earth and Earth in orbit around the Sun, helping you understand patterns in space and celestial movements.

Bringing It All Together

Now, you have built a foundation in math, reading, and science that connects to the real world. You can solve larger math problems, understand and summarize texts, and explain natural patterns like tides and eclipses. These skills prepare you for even more advanced topics, such as fractions, decimals, critical reading, and deeper space science. Remember, everything you’ve learned works together — math helps you calculate and measure, reading helps you understand and think critically, and science helps you see patterns in the world and universe around you.
