Math

Adding & Subtracting with Larger Numbers

Now that you understand place value, you can use it to add and subtract bigger numbers. For example, when adding 345 + 276, you start with the ones place,5+6 (carry the 1) then the tens 4+7+1 (carry that 1), and then the hundreds,3 + 2 + 1. Place value helps you line up the numbers correctly and understand why carrying or borrowing works.

Reading

Main Ideas and Supporting Details

After learning to read paragraphs, the next step is understanding the main idea and supporting details. The main idea is what the paragraph is mostly about. Supporting details are facts or examples that explain or prove the main idea. For example, if a paragraph is about penguins’ swimming skills, details about how they dive, slide on ice, and catch fish support that idea. Learning to identify main ideas and supporting details helps you summarize texts and answer comprehension questions more accurately.

Science

Moon Phases and Tides

Next, you will connect moon phases to real-world effects on Earth. As the Moon orbits Earth, its phases change, and these changes affect ocean tides. High tides occur when the Moon’s gravity pulls on Earth’s water, and low tides happen when the pull is weaker. You will learn to identify which phase of the Moon causes spring tides (very high and very low tides) and neap tides (moderate tides). Understanding this helps explain patterns in the ocean and shows how Earth and Moon are connected.
